## Description (4000 chars max, pure ASCII, no emoji)

NavFocus is the first navigation app built specifically for people with ADHD. If you have ever missed a turn because you zoned out, forgotten which stop is yours on the bus, or felt overwhelmed by complex directions, NavFocus was made for you.

WHY NAVFOCUS IS DIFFERENT

Standard navigation apps give you one reminder per turn and expect you to stay focused. But for millions of adults with ADHD, that single reminder is not enough. NavFocus fills this gap with repeated, customizable reminders that keep you on track from start to finish.

KEY FEATURES

- Repeated Turn Reminders: Set your preferred frequency and receive ongoing alerts until you complete each turn. No more missed exits or forgotten turns.
- Haptic Feedback Patterns: Distinct vibration patterns for left turns, right turns, and arrivals so you can feel the direction without looking at the screen.
- Voice Navigation: Clear, spoken turn-by-turn instructions that repeat at your chosen interval. Adjustable volume to match your preference.
- Transit Stop Alerts: Set how many stops before your destination you want to be alerted. Get haptic and voice reminders so you never miss your bus or train stop.
- Live Compass: A real-time compass showing your heading and the direction to your next turn, perfect for walking navigation.
- Route Search: Search for any destination and get driving, walking, or transit routes with full ADHD-optimized guidance.
- Customizable Settings: Adjust reminder frequency from 10 seconds to 2 minutes, control volume, enable or disable haptics and voice independently.

DESIGNED FOR ADHD

Every feature in NavFocus is designed to reduce cognitive load and support executive function challenges common in ADHD:

- Minimal, distraction-free interface
- Clear, large turn instruction panels
- No unnecessary visual clutter
- Settings that are simple and intuitive
- Haptic patterns that are distinct and easy to learn

WHO NAVFOCUS IS FOR

- Adults with ADHD who struggle with turn-by-turn navigation
- Anyone who zones out while driving and misses turns
- Transit riders who lose track of their stop
- People who find standard navigation apps insufficient
- Neurodivergent individuals who need extra reminders

NAVFOCUS VS STANDARD NAVIGATION

Standard apps: One reminder per turn. Miss it and you are off route.
NavFocus: Repeated reminders at your chosen frequency until you complete the turn. Plus haptic patterns, voice guidance, and transit alerts all working together.

PRIVACY FIRST

NavFocus does not collect or store your location data on any server. Location is processed on your device in real time. No third-party analytics, no advertising trackers, no data selling. Your navigation stays private.

Download NavFocus today and never miss a turn again.
